# Privacy notice

Applies to Android **0.4.4-pro-preview**. Updated **October 21, 2026**.

[简体中文](PRIVACY.md) · [Home](../README.en.md)

## Your archive stays on your device

Item names, photos, receipt recognition content, locations, categories, tags, notes, drafts, and preferences are stored on the current device. This version has no account system, author-operated cloud storage, ads, or usage analytics service. It does not automatically upload your archive to the author. Photo and receipt recognition use engines and models supplied with the app and run locally.

When you choose to export, print, share a file, or send information by email, it is passed to the system function or service you select. These actions are not automatic cloud synchronization. Do not post backups, original receipts, or screenshots with sensitive locations publicly.

## Photos, files, and clipboard

Taking a photo uses the system camera. Selecting photos and saving backups use system selection interfaces. You choose which files to provide or save. The app does not request phone, contacts, geolocation, or whole-library photo access. An item's storage location is a record you enter, not GPS tracking.

Copy buttons write the selected device code, email address, or purchase information to the system clipboard. Pasting an activation code is initiated by you; the app does not scan other apps' clipboard history. Handle clipboard contents appropriately for your device environment.

## Device activation

Android Pro activation uses the system's scoped Android ID, hashed locally into a device code beginning with `STA1-`. The app does not read an IMEI or MAC address or request phone permissions. Activation credentials are stored locally. Signatures and device matching are checked on the device without an author-operated server.

You do not need to send the author a device code to use the free features. For a Pro purchase or activation support, you choose to email the device code so the author can issue the matching activation code. It is not your item archive, but avoid posting it publicly.

## Purchases, email, and feedback

Payment is handled by the WeChat or Alipay service you choose. The app does not automatically receive payment confirmation or read your payment password, bank card details, or payment app history. For a purchase, follow the in-app instructions and send the device code and payment order number to **zhiandzhi@gmail.com**. You do not need to provide a payment password or complete archive backup.

The author uses the email address, device code, order information, and issue details you provide to verify payment, deliver activation codes, and handle support. Email, payment services, and GitHub process information you submit to them under their own practices and privacy settings. Do not post device codes, activation codes, order numbers, or personal photos in public Issues.

For questions or requests about information you have sent by email, contact the address above. There is no automated account deletion or online order-management portal.

## Deletion and backups

You can delete items in the app. Clearing app data or uninstalling deletes the local archive and preferences. Backups, exported files, photo copies, and emails you have saved or sent elsewhere are not automatically deleted; manage those separately.

Backups contain readable item data and photos. The app does not currently password-encrypt backup files. Keep them somewhere you trust. If you upload a backup to a cloud drive, that service handles the file you selected. Full item backups do not include Pro activation or unfinished drafts.
