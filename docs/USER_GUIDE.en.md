# User guide

[简体中文](USER_GUIDE.md) · [Home](../README.en.md)

## Add your first item

Tap the bottom add-item button and enter a name. Photos and other details are optional. Expand the additional details to record location, purchase date, price, category, tags, and notes. Categories can be customized.

When leaving an unfinished entry, keep or discard the draft. You can continue a saved draft next time. Drafts are not yet part of the item archive and are not included in a full archive backup. After saving, use the add-another action to optionally reuse the previous location and category.

## Find and organize

Search names, locations, and tags, and filter by category or status. Links in item details let you find other items with the same location, category, or tag.

Long-press an item on the home screen or in search results, or tap the multi-select action. Review the selected items before moving, favoriting, or updating their status. Swipe right to open deletion confirmation. Deletion is not an undoable archive action. Mark items you no longer own as departed to retain their records. Back up before permanently deleting items.

Photos can be enlarged, browsed, reordered, and selected as the cover. Read the confirmation before replacing or removing a photo. Removing the only photo leaves the item record intact.

## Locations, boxes, and QR labels

Manage locations under Me and select multiple items inside a box to move them. Scanning an existing storage label is free, but the corresponding location data must exist on your device. A QR label is not a public cloud inventory or a way to share its contents with every person who scans it. Restore the same backup on a new device before using its labels.

## Backup and restore

1. Export a backup under Me and finish the system save flow.
2. Check that the file actually exists, then keep a separate copy somewhere you trust. Opening a download or save dialog is not proof that the file was saved.
3. Before restoring, review the contents and choose whether to keep existing records, replace conflicts, or keep copies. Changes are applied only after confirmation.
4. Replacing conflicts overwrites the corresponding details and photos. Back up the current archive first.

A full backup includes item details, photos, categories, locations, and storage label identifiers. It does not include unfinished drafts or Pro activation. CSV files, printouts, and reports are not substitutes for a full backup. Backup files are readable files and are not password-encrypted by the app. Store them privately; do not upload them publicly.

## Known limitations

- Android APK only; no iOS app, account, automatic cloud backup, or automatic device synchronization.
- Prices are recorded in CNY. Switching to English does not convert currencies or translate your item data.
- Offline recognition can be wrong and its speed depends on your phone and photos. Manual entry remains available.
- Uninstalling, clearing data, losing the phone, or device failure can destroy local data. Export backups regularly.
- Pro activation is for one device and is not transferred by restoring an item backup. Contact the author before changing devices, flashing a ROM, or performing a factory reset.
- Some older systems may need an Android System WebView update. Include your version and reproduction steps when reporting an issue, but do not post a complete backup in public Issues.
