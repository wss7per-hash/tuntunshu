# Stash · 囤囤鼠

A little home for the things you love.

An Android app for recording your belongings, saving favorites, and remembering where things are stored. Keep photos and details for your headphones, backpack, camera, and everyday treasures on your own device.

## What you can do

- **Record belongings:** photos, names, categories, locations, purchase dates, prices, tags, and notes. Fill in optional details later.
- **Save favorites:** mark items you love and track whether they are in use, worth revisiting, ready to part with, or no longer owned.
- **Find things:** search names, locations, and tags, with category and status filters.
- **Organize multiple items:** long-press to select, move items or update their status in bulk, and swipe right to open a deletion confirmation.
- **Manage photos and drafts:** view larger photos, change the cover and order, and keep an unfinished single-item entry as a local draft.
- **Keep your own backup:** export the complete archive with photos and review conflicts before restoring.

Basic recording, search, category and location management, scanning existing storage labels, and full backup and restore are free. No account, cloud subscription, or ads.

## Pro

Activate Pro inside the same app. There is no separate Pro APK.

| Feature | Where it helps |
| --- | --- |
| Photo suggestions | Suggest a generic item name and category while adding an item; review before applying |
| Receipt assistance | Extract details from a receipt and apply the fields you confirm |
| Storage label creation | Create, save, and print QR labels for locations and boxes |
| Advanced insights and lists | Explore value, categories, and years; filter and export lists |

Current price: **CNY ¥9.9, one-time purchase for one device**. Payment is verified manually and an activation code is sent by email. **Activation is not delivered automatically immediately after payment.** No recurring charge or cloud synchronization is included.

Photo and receipt recognition run on the device. Results can be wrong and need review. Photo suggestions focus on generic names such as “headphones,” “backpack,” and “camera”; brand/model recognition is not guaranteed. The app does not infer a price or storage location from an item's appearance. Your basic archive remains accessible without Pro.

See [Pro and purchasing](docs/PRO.en.md). The same APK serves users worldwide. Foreign-currency checkout is not currently provided. If the available payment methods do not work for you, email the author before paying.
